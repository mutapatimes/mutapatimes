Meet Dr Rumbi
On the frontlines of healthcare, and the strategy table. A cool July afternoon at the Wellcome Collection with the Zimbabwean-British doctor and founder of the women's-health venture Glacie Health.

She is a practising NHS doctor working in general practice, an educator, and the founder and CEO of Glacie Health, a venture built to close the gap between good intentions and the care women actually receive. She had come to London to teach at the Zimbabwean Embassy, part of a broader mission: to gather what the West knows and carry it, patiently, back to the women who need it.

We talked about calling, about education as a form of medicine, about Black maternal health, and about why the smartest move a Zimbabwean-trained doctor can make might be going home.
“When you've trained here and you go back, there is an advantage waiting for you. It is, quite literally, your competitive advantage.”
