South Africans commonly refer to being “blacklisted” by credit bureaus in the mistaken belief that if they behave irresponsibly with credit, such as missing a debt repayment, their names go into a “little black book” and they are automatically denied access to credit in future. But that is not how it works, writes Martin Hesse.
