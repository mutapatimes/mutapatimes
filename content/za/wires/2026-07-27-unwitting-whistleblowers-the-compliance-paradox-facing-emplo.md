South African employers are required to make a good faith effort to ascertain whether a foreign national’s status authorises the particular employment concerned. Verification routes exist, but there is no universally understood or standardised employer process explaining which route to use, what evidential value it carries, or whether formal verification may expose a trusted employee to serious immigration consequences.
