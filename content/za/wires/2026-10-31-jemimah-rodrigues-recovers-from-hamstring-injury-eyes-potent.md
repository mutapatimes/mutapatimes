Jemimah Rodrigues confirmed her recovery from a hamstring injury that left her sidelined for three months. She sustained the injury while playing for Southern Brave in August and missed significant tournaments. Following rehabilitation, Rodrigues announced on Instagram that her hamstring is now fit for action. She aims for a return during India's multi-format tour of South Africa in December.
