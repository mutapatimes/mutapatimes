The era of reaping excessive profits purely by leveraging the "American brand label" has officially drawn to a full close. For decades, the massive Chinese consumer market was long regarded as a highly lucrative gold mine by U.S.-origin brands, where the staggering purchasing power of its 1.4 billion population lured top global industry giants ranging from Nike, Starbucks to General Motors to rush for large-scale market expansion and seize growth opportunities in the region.
