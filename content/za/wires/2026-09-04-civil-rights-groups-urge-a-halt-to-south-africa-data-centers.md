Civil rights groups in South Africa are calling for a pause on building more data centers until their use of water, land and electricity is investigated. The country is positioning itself as Africa's main data hub, attracting global tech companies like Amazon and Microsoft. But concerns have been raised about transparency and the environmental impact of these projects. The South African human rights watchdog has received numerous submissions seeking more transparency. The commission noted significant growth in data centers but highlighted important human rights and environmental considerations. Some experts emphasize the need for clear rules to ensure transparency and balance competing interests.
