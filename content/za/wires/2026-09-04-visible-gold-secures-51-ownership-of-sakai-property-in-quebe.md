TSX-V-listed Visible Gold Mines has entered into an amended agreement with fellow-listed Morocco Strategic Minerals Corporation to acquire the Sakai property in the James Bay Territory of Québec. The company has concurrently completed the closing of the transaction, acquiring an initial 51% interest in the property, and become its operator.
