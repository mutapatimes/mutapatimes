Social media platforms like Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube are unwittingly facilitating a sophisticated illegal wildlife trade that threatens global biodiversity. A recent study published in Nature Reviews Biodiversity reveals alarming statistics, including over 1,600 primates advertised for sale online, alongside a vast black market for various species. This crisis calls for comprehensive strategies that engage local communities and enhance regulatory frameworks.
