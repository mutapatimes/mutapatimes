Financial services company Aditya Birla Capital will enter India's gold loan market and plans to open about 1 000 dedicated branches over the next three years to expand its secured lending business, the financial services firm said on Thursday. Elevated gold prices, supported by safe-haven demand linked to the Iran war, have increased the value of jewellery pledged as collateral, boosting growth prospects for India's gold loan industry.
